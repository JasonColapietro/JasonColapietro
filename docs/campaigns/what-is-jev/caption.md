## Caption

What is Jev? 🧭

Most AI writes you a paragraph. Jev gives you a decision.

Jev is the first model from TypeSafe AI, which came out of stealth on Sept 15 with a $40M seed round led by DCVC. TypeSafe calls it a "System One" model, after the fast, intuitive thinking Daniel Kahneman described.

How it's different:
01 Typed answers, not prose: choices, scores and extracted values your code can use directly
02 A 0–1 confidence score on every answer, so your software knows when to act and when to escalate
03 Fast: 70–500 ms end to end, per TypeSafe
04 Built for the decisions inside software: classification, routing, scoring, extraction

It's led by Diogo Almeida, who spent about four years at OpenAI on RLHF, InstructGPT, ChatGPT and GPT-4. Jev is in limited early access now.

Our take: chat was the demo. Decisions are the infrastructure. 🔗 Link in bio.
