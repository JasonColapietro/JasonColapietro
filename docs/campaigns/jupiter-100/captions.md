## Hashtags

Use 12–18 per post and rotate, so five days of posts don't look identical. Consider putting the
local-niche block in the first comment and keeping the broad tags on the caption itself.

**Jupiter / northern Palm Beach** — local-niche, low volume, highest intent. These are the tags that
actually reach owners.
`#JupiterFL #JupiterFlorida #ShopJupiter #JupiterSmallBusiness #TequestaFL #PalmBeachGardens #Abacoa #JupiterInletColony`

**County-wide** — mid volume, regional reach.
`#PalmBeachCounty #PBCBusiness #SouthFloridaBusiness #561`

**Small business** — high volume, broad reach, lower intent. Good for saves and shares.
`#SmallBusinessOwner #ShopLocal #SupportLocalBusiness #SmallBizMarketing`

**SEO / web** — mid-to-high volume, category discovery.
`#LocalSEO #WebDesign #SEOTips`

**AI / build in public** — where the founder and builder audience lives.
`#AIAgents #BuildInPublic #GoogleCloudPartner`

## Before posting

Two lines are worth a sanity check, since this is a public claim about the business:

- **"data APIs only available to Google Cloud partners"** — make sure the specific APIs you have in
  mind are actually partner-gated, and that your Partner Advantage agreement permits using them to
  deliver client work. The repo record supports Partner Advantage membership since September 4, 2026;
  it does not by itself establish which APIs that unlocks.
- **"gathering competitor data based on scraping of public-facing sites"** — accurate to what the
  agents do, but stated plainly in a public post it invites argument. Consider "gathering publicly
  available competitor data" instead, which says the same thing without leading on the method.

Neither is a correctness problem with the campaign. Both are just phrasing that will be quoted back.
