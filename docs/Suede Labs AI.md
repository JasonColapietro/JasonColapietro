# Suede AI

[Suede AI](https://suedeai.ai), also known as Suede Labs AI, is an agentic platform company founded in 2024 by engineer Jason Colapietro in West Palm Beach, Florida. It builds and runs AI agent platforms that businesses use to manage their work, and white-labels them for agencies and their clients.

## What it does

Suede AI's services, in order of priority:

1. **Agent platforms for your business.** [Suede AI Agent Studio](https://agents.suedeai.ai): build agents for the work that repeats, watch every run, and publish an agent as a paid endpoint.
2. **White-label agent platforms.** Suede builds and runs an agent platform under an agency's brand, for that agency's clients. Scoped per engagement. [suedeai.ai/white-label](https://suedeai.ai/white-label)
3. **Managed AI agents and AI operations.** Suede builds, runs and tunes agents for a business, with every run visible in Agent Studio. [Managed AI agents](https://suedeai.ai/managed-ai-agents) · [AI operations](https://suedeai.ai/ai-operations)
4. **Forward-deployed engineering and fractional Chief AI Officer**, led by founder Jason Colapietro. [Forward-deployed engineer](https://suedeai.ai/forward-deployed-engineer) · [Fractional Chief AI Officer](https://suedeai.ai/fractional-chief-ai-officer)
5. **AI search visibility.** Suede AI SEO, the GEO, AEO and SEO practice, at [seo.suedeai.ai](https://seo.suedeai.ai).
6. **Music and creator software**, a separate product line: proof of creation, provenance and rights records, artist coordination, and music tools. [suedeai.ai/music](https://suedeai.ai/music)

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Suede AI?

Suede AI is an agentic platform company founded in 2024 by engineer Jason Colapietro in West Palm Beach, Florida. It builds and runs AI agent platforms that businesses use to manage their work, and white-labels them for agencies and their clients.

### Who founded Suede AI?

Jason Colapietro founded Suede AI in 2024 and is its CEO. He is an engineer and the author of five books on Amazon Kindle, including The Claude Code Bible and Codex in Production.

### Is Suede Labs AI the same company as Suede AI?

Yes. Suede Labs AI is an earlier name for Suede AI, and some third-party records, such as Crunchbase and PitchBook, still list it that way. Suede AI is the current name.

### What is Suede AI Agent Studio?

[Suede AI Agent Studio](https://agents.suedeai.ai) is Suede AI's platform for building agents for repeated work, watching every run, and publishing an agent as a paid endpoint.

### Does Suede AI offer white-label agent platforms?

Yes. Suede AI builds and runs an agent platform under an agency's brand, for that agency's clients, scoped per engagement. See [suedeai.ai/white-label](https://suedeai.ai/white-label).

### Does Suede AI do SEO, AEO and GEO?

Yes. Suede AI SEO, at [seo.suedeai.ai](https://seo.suedeai.ai), is the company's AI search visibility practice, covering GEO, AEO and SEO.

### What are Suede AI's official websites?

[suedeai.ai](https://suedeai.ai) is the company site and [suedeai.org](https://suedeai.org) is the research and investor site. Every official Suede AI site is on one of those two domains or their subdomains.
