---

## What I'm building

As AI makes media easier to generate, remix, license, and distribute, creators need a stronger ownership layer underneath all of it. **Suede Labs AI** makes IP easier to register, verify, license, route, and monetize — across product surfaces, multiple chains, and agent-driven commerce.

Music is the initial wedge. The thesis applies to any creator-owned IP: songs, video, likeness, training data, generative outputs.

## Current focus

- **Programmable IP** — register, verify, and route rights for creator-owned media
- **Provenance** — verifiable authorship for AI-native creative assets
- **Licensing** — sync, sample, master, and likeness workflows on-chain
- **Royalty infrastructure** — on-chain splits and cross-chain settlement
- **Agent commerce** — x402 paid endpoints and ACP-ready surfaces for AI agents
- **Multichain rails** — Base, Solana, Ethereum, Avalanche, omnichain
